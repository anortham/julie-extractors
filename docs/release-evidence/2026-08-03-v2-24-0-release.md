## What shipped

Cold-scan performance: memory-aware bulk cache (total/8 clamped [512 MiB,
8 GiB], safe probes, `JULIE_BULK_CACHE_KIB` override), savepoint-free bulk
resolution (new `BulkResolutionFailed` abort semantics on bulk first builds
only; WAL delta paths unchanged), and three retired secondary indexes
(`idx_identifiers_path`, `idx_identifiers_file_line_name`,
`idx_reference_sites_span`) with an idempotent drop migration on open. On the
dotnet/runtime yardstick: wall 76.3 → 23.7 min (3.2–4.1× run-to-run),
artifact 22.84 → 20.41 GiB, with identical row counts across all 24 domains
and identical resolution outcome aggregates. Full notes in
`docs/release-notes/v2.24.0.md`.
