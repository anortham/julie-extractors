## WHAT

Implemented and independently reviewed direct scoped resolution-delta finalization. Scoped one-file work now carries the existing base-relative prior delta, recomputes only selected/touched/removed closure rows with keyed base access, enforces scoped identifier totality and stale-target safety, preserves gap/tombstone semantics, and leaves forced-full/rebase as the exact oracle.

## WHY

The public one-file replay spent 5.562 seconds in `finish_exact`, materializing and validating the whole exact corpus after a small change. An earlier streaming draft was rejected because it still traversed the full corpus. This implementation removes that corpus-sized finalization work.

## HOW

Added selected-version current/prior reads, keyed removed-source/removed-target base reads, prior-delta carry-forward, batched target validation, declared/duplicate gap validation, exclusive resolver/diff timing arithmetic, and test-only row/query telemetry. Tests include a cold unselected corpus, non-empty prior delta, touched-over-prior replacements, pending carry/tombstones, removed-target invalidation, totality failure, gap corruption, rebase/crash/timing, and exact forced-full parity.

## IMPACT

Independent semantic review is clean. SQL review confirms no full scan or N+1 and its final missing query-count assertions are green. Architecture/rebase review is clean with benchmark risk: in-memory maps are proportional to prior delta plus selected/removed closure, so the guarded Linux replay must prove the 600 MiB peak PSS gate before this approach is accepted.
