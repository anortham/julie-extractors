## Confirmed repeated ready-base validation owns the remaining one-file wall

### WHAT

A final low-overhead aggregate replay at producer `9654cdaf` split the previously unexplained setup and confirmed the same ready base is fully validated three times during an ordinary scoped, non-rebase resolve.

### WHY

The public one-file wall remained above the 5-second gate after scoped finalization. We needed exclusive attribution before changing validation or adding any cache/index.

### HOW

The temporary default-off probe measured one isolated public replay: `pre_resolution_setup=1,489,477 us`, with `convergence_bind_pin=1,477,013 us` (99.163% of setup), and `rebase_decision=1,571,742 us`. Source mapping proves both paths call `ResolutionBaseCatalog::find_ready`; `PriorOverlayReader::open` performs a third `ResolutionBaseReader::open` inside the 2.174-second resolution bucket. Each pass repeats integrity/row checks, complete-file SHA-256, root checks, and target validation on the same pinned immutable ready base.

### IMPACT

The next permanent slice is request-local reuse of one validated ready-base proof across binding, prior-overlay opening, and rebase decision. It must use an opaque validated token, retain cheap catalog identity checks plus pin/fence/CAS publication, retain strict standalone validation APIs as fallbacks, and add an end-to-end count guard proving one base validation pass. No process-global cache or corruption-check disablement is accepted.
