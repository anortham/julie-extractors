## WHAT

Implemented Task 8O's opaque `ResolutionValidatedBase` proof. Binding validates the ready base once and returns the proof with the binding and pin. Resolve transports a clone into the scratch session and exact rebase policy. Prior-overlay opening and rebase decision compare the current catalog identity before using the proof; mismatches fall back to the existing strict reader/catalog validation.
