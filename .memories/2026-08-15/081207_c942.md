## WHAT

The exact-commit guarded Linux one-file replay at Julie `9654cdaf` completed successfully and cut wall time from 14.506 s to 6.890 s. Resolution fell from 7.978 s to 2.177 s and direct scoped diff/finalization measured 837 ms.

## WHY

This replay was the acceptance gate for replacing full exact finalization with direct scoped delta composition. It needed to prove correctness, timing, and the map-based implementation's 600 MiB memory risk on production-volume captured data.

## HOW

Built the exact Release producer (`julie-extract 2.33.2`, SHA-256 `47866ae7…b44cd`) and ran the guarded `producer.resolve.one_file` workload against isolated captured inputs with a fresh request/idempotency identity. The stock identity first failed safely with `idempotency_conflict`; that attempt is preserved separately and is not performance evidence.

## IMPACT

Exact scoped semantics, one-file scope, no timeout, and 223 MB peak PSS all pass. The map-based approach is below the 600 MiB hard cap. The 5 s wall gate still fails by 1.890 s. Since `resolution + diff` is 3.014 s, the remaining 3.876 s is post-diff residual work, narrowing the next diagnosis to delta publication/gap handling/terminal persistence rather than tree-sitter or resolver computation.
