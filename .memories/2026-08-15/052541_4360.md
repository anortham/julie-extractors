## WHAT

Implemented and independently reviewed chunk-batched propagation coverage for scoped store resolution. The resolver now submits each read-stable phase chunk once, and the Store adapter checks pending and materialized relationship coverage through one reader with bounded, paginated batch queries. Added real Store fixtures for mixed coverage, duplicates, multiple chunks, nullable line fallback, ambiguous/unsupported materialized locators, pending scratch precedence, frozen-prior override, and adversarial same-name cardinality.

## WHY

The corrected production profile attributed 4.230 seconds of an 11.508-second scoped resolution phase to 2,151 scalar propagation checks that opened/read coverage state 4,302 times. The first batch implementation still allowed same-name join multiplication and had a nullable-line mismatch against the scalar oracle; independent reviews caught both before commit.

## HOW

- Added a covered-identifier-set batch seam with a scalar default for non-Store adapters.
- Deduplicated chunk keys and preserved duplicate caller membership without positional result coupling.
- Batched pending candidates by distinct `(version_id,name)`, loaded identifier locators once, paged each pending row once, and applied exact byte/span or line matching in memory.
- Preserved scratch-touched precedence over the frozen prior overlay and propagated overlay fallback errors.
- Restricted materialized candidates by exact locator plus uniqueness and supported relationship kind.
- Kept the scalar path, SQLite schema, CLI/MCP contracts, and finalization behavior unchanged.

## IMPACT

This removes the measured per-identifier coverage N+1 while preserving exact resolution semantics. It is the first of two measured scoped-resolution recovery slices; full exact finalization remains the other 5.562-second owner and will be addressed only after the public one-file replay quantifies this slice.
