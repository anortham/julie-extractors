## Go literal quality slice reviewed and ready to commit

### WHAT
Reviewed and accepted the delegated Go literal slice after one fix pass. Go now has fixture-backed `kind_coverage.literals.supported: ["other"]` evidence in `fixtures/extraction/go/basic`.

### WHY
The branch is raising language data quality across the matrix. Go had existing literal unit coverage but no golden fixture/capability evidence, leaving `go.literals` as an open quality debt.

### HOW
- Go basic fixture now includes `observeRun("worker-run", id)` and `http.Get("https://api.example.com/workers/status")`.
- Golden output emits two `literals` rows with useful carriers: local helper `observeRun` and dotted stdlib call `http.Get`.
- The first delegated version used `println`, which generated a bogus pending relationship to a builtin; review rejected that. The fixed version uses a local helper and has no new pending relationships.
- `fixtures/extraction/capabilities.json` closes only Go `literals` with supported kind `other`.
- Findings doc adds the Phase 2 Go literal slice and updates scorecard counts.

### IMPACT
- `quality_bar_debts` dropped from 92 to 91.
- Literal fixture-proven coverage increased from 11/36 to 12/36.
- This validates the bounded cheaper-worker workflow for one-language/domain slices, as long as lead review checks generated golden evidence and relationship noise.
