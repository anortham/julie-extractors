## WHAT
Measured all 22 reporting counts against immutable e11fe30a release output on 14-file and 1,315-file source snapshots.

## WHY
The approved follow-up asked whether whole-artifact counting makes incremental commands scale poorly.

## HOW
Warm command runs used fresh copies of seeded artifacts; count-query attribution used the same bundled SQLite/rusqlite version. On 1,688,470 rows, count-loop p95 was 10.472 ms, update 544.041 ms, delete 534.918 ms. Exact samples and reproducible commands are in docs/findings/2026-09-27-incremental-reporting-cost.md.

## IMPACT
Keep exact reporting counts. At about 2% of these measured command times, the counts do not justify a durable cache or approximate totals. Receiver correctness implementation continues independently.
