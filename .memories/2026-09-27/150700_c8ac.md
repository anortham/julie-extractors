## WHAT
Implemented producer guards for scan/update/delete/rebind and transaction-time checks in ArtifactWriter/rebind. Capability fingerprint v2 includes the extractor semantic contract. Force can adopt an old generation without unlinking the compatible artifact.

## WHY
Partial writes could stamp current producer metadata while keeping older facts. Generation changes must replace facts and metadata together.

## HOW
Reuse binary/parser/capability identity, not a new schema field. Refuse all partial mismatches; only Force may change generation. Abort changed-generation force on discovery/extraction errors, including a new unreadable file. Keep the old generation on rollback and handle empty scans. Lead acceptance tests cover all three fields and four verbs, read compatibility, unchanged/deleted files, empty scans and SQL-trigger rollback. Worker tests cover writer races, failed rows, staged non-force rejection and reuse after force.
