## Closed TODO 15 parser reuse after profiling

- **WHAT:** Profiled the parser setup concern and documented the decision in `TODO.md` without adding a parser cache.
- **WHY:** TODO 15 required profiling before implementation. The measured overhead did not justify adding thread-local parser state.
- **HOW:** Temporarily added a focused ignored profiler comparing 2,000 tiny Rust parses through `parse_for_language` against one configured parser reused for all parses. Also added a red parser-count test to confirm the current behavior configures once per call, then removed the temporary scaffolding after recording evidence.
- **IMPACT:** Avoids speculative mutable thread-local parser caching. Keeps the parser path simple until a real scan baseline shows parser setup is a meaningful bottleneck.
