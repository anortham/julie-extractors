## Moved extraction-owned language policy

- **WHAT:** Added standalone `languages/*.toml` files containing only `[literal_carriers]`, plus a `julie_extractors::language_policy` loader and literal classification gate.
- **WHY:** Literal carrier policy directly affects artifact rows by setting `literals.kind` and dropping non-carrier literal bloat before persistence. Search, embedding, watcher, scoring, and old Julie test-role policy are outside the product boundary.
- **HOW:** Split old Julie language TOML down to literal carriers only, embedded them with `include_str!`, added JSX/TSX aliases, lowercased carrier sets, and kept exact/last-segment carrier matching. Added tests that fail if non-extraction TOML sections are copied or files are not embedded.
- **IMPACT:** Task 4 of the migration plan is ready to commit; artifact writer work can call the crate policy instead of depending on old Julie `src/` pipeline code.
