## Rejected Qt macro scan cleanup

### WHAT

Measured the proposed narrow cleanup that skips duplicate outer line/prefix/class checks for non-class Qt macro-scan identifiers.

### WHY

Final Qt C++ corpus p95 was 0.86s, so the optimization could remain only if it improved the same fixed workload.

### HOW

Built candidate binary SHA-256 `530b27717083e858b9bdc5fc5ca985d00701442e0091f9536615210a6497e0ce`; with one warm scan and five quiet-machine scans of the pinned Kirigami Qt C++ corpus, samples were 0.87, 0.85, 0.86, 0.86, and 0.87 seconds (p95 0.87s). The baseline final binary was p95 0.86s.

### IMPACT

The cleanup does not improve the required metric and must be reverted. `docs/reviews/qt-release-performance.json` preserves the candidate checksum, raw path, samples, baseline, and rejection decision while retaining the pre-optimization final evidence.
