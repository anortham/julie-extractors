### WHY
The released Qt support already recognized those tokens as property-boundary keywords but discarded their values, losing Qt designer/runtime semantics from the artifact.

### IMPACT
Qt property rows now retain all standard value-bearing attributes already recognized by the parser. The forthcoming scanner performance patch still needs C++ focused verification.
