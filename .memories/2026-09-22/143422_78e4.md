## v3.3.1 post-Claude corpus verification

### WHAT

Added `docs/reviews/2026-09-22-v3-3-1-post-claude-corpus.json`, separate from the v3.3.0 historical performance record. It contains pinned corpus facts, binary checksums, quality comparisons, diagnostic-span comparison, declaration-family checks, raw timings, and limitations.

### WHY

Claude review fixes restored broad Qt declaration preprocessing and corrected QML ownership. The release candidate needed evidence that the changes do not introduce new corpus diagnostics or failures, and needed an honest performance comparison.

### HOW

Compared v3.3.0 and frozen v3.3.1 candidate SHA-256 `ceaa8e391f6658d4f63e4fa73f776dfabcc43076bbd83edc74b922559df160f6` on pinned Plasma `a45871a7b24f68bed09df3c7282effc86c42535c`, Kirigami `b5ff40fa6ada29bfc4e95f1c80be28e469fa83d5`, and Quickshell `fae96f1a5b7f53de9b7e40e5b53c0b7a2e97b1d7`. Plasma has zero failures and diagnostics 298 to 297; Kirigami C++ and both QML diagnostic sets are exact matches or unchanged. Sampled restored declaration sites have no diagnostics. Quiet wall-time p95 rises: Plasma C++ 7.62 to 8.68 seconds, Kirigami C++ 0.77 to 0.88, Kirigami QML 1.22 to 1.25, Quickshell QML 0.17 to 0.18. No causal claim is made. The corrected macro long-line p95 remains 3.73 to 2.21 seconds (40.8% lower).

### IMPACT

The candidate has source-backed corpus evidence for the restored Qt declaration family and QML repair, plus bounded, candid performance evidence. No unprofiled QML performance change was made.
