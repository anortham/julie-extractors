## Final Qt/QML release performance evidence

### WHAT

Recorded the final pinned release and fixed-binary measurements in `docs/reviews/qt-release-performance.json`. The final binary hash is `f00f863ecaebff89e31396ff41dc8b21058db64ac143a86ea0e777f0e3a72111`.

### WHY

The review needed reproducible evidence for the v3.2.0/v3.3.0 quality push and proof that the discovered Qt C++ performance regression was actually corrected.

### HOW

On a quiet machine, each workload used one warm scan then five fresh SQLite artifact scans at full level and one worker. Final p95 wall time: Kirigami QML 1.25s, Quickshell 0.17s, and Kirigami Qt C++ 0.86s. Interleaved adversarial scans of one 169KB, 10,000-member one-line Qt class measured v3.3.0 at 3.46s p95 and the final current binary at 2.24s p95. The correction passes a precomputed line start to `qt_macros::line_prefix` instead of rescanning from byte zero per identifier.

### IMPACT

The macro-path p95 is 35.3% lower in an interleaved final comparison. Final Kirigami Qt C++ extraction has 102 scanned files, zero failures, 31 parse diagnostics, 4,271 symbols, and 154 relationships; the diagnostic count matches v3.3.0 after the macro-vocabulary regression was repaired. Release-level raw samples, artifact protocol, source commits, binary checksums, exact byte totals, and limitations are retained in the tracked JSON evidence.
