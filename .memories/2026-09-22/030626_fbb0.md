## wave 8 R7+R8: the whole declared return type, and `final` is trailing

**R7.** `extract_basic_return_type` read only the first `primitive_type`, `type_identifier`, `qualified_identifier` or `auto` child of one node, so it dropped the pointer star, the reference ampersand, the leading `const`, and every `template_type` and namespace-qualified type. A free function declaration lost its return type completely, because the symbol is built from the bare `function_declarator` and the type lives on the parent declaration.

Renamed it to `declared_return_type` and rebuilt it:

- `return_type_declaration` walks up from the `function_declarator` through the `pointer_declarator` and `reference_declarator` wrappers to the `field_declaration`, `declaration` or `function_definition`, collecting one `*`, `&` or `&&` per wrapper.
- `RETURN_TYPE_KINDS` now covers `template_type`, `scoped_type_identifier`, `sized_type_specifier`, `dependent_type`, `decltype` and the `struct`/`enum`/`union` specifiers.
- The leading `type_qualifier` children before the type node are kept.
- The result is the text to prepend to the name and ends in the mark (`QQuickItem *`) or in a space (`void `), so Qt style attaches the star to the name. The two signature builders and the friend-operator signature in `declarations.rs` stopped adding their own space.

**R8.** `final` was missing from the method modifier list, so `void paint() final;` rendered as `void paint()`. Added it to the list and to `TRAILING_SPECIFIERS`, beside `override`.

**Impact:** `QQuickItem *contentItem() const`, `const QString &name() const`, `QString &&take()`, `static X *instance()`, `QList<int> *items()`, `const char *free_name()`, `X &&mover()`, `void paint() final`.

**Finding:** no cpp or c golden fixture declares a pointer, reference, template or qualified return type, so the goldens did not change. The corpus measurement is the only wide check for R7.
