## Repaired final Qt C++ corpus regression

### WHAT
- Restored macro-preprocessing coverage for `Q_IMPORT_PLUGIN`, `Q_GLOBAL_STATIC`, `Q_GHS_KEEP_REFERENCE`, and `Q_DECL_*` after the declaration whitelist caused 15 new Kirigami parse diagnostics.
- Added a dedicated `Q_UNUSED` rewrite that removes the macro wrapper but keeps its argument expression and turns the outer close into a semicolon, retaining nested call facts even when source omits a semicolon.

### WHY
The narrowed Qt declaration vocabulary regressed corpus diagnostics from 31 to 46. Blanket blanking `Q_UNUSED` would have repaired parsing but lost argument call identifiers.

### HOW
Used performance's exact corpus locations to add only the missing declaration macros. The `Q_UNUSED(helper())` regression test verifies diagnostic-free canonical extraction and retains the `helper` call at its original offset.

### IMPACT
The repaired corpus returns to v3.3 parity: 102 files, 0 failures, 31 diagnostics, 4271 symbols, and 154 relationships. Identifier count rises from 16689 to 16780 because macro-use calls are retained. Focused C++ macro/symbol suites and C++ golden verification pass.
