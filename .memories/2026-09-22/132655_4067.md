## WHAT

Completed QML relationship scope corrections and removed the stale QML ownership known-limit documentation.

## WHY

## HOW

The pending-call walk now reuses the concrete caller and scope resolvers, filters candidates to callable symbols, and emits a structured pending edge when a known target is outside lexical component scope. Function ownership now matches declaration start bytes. Added canonical coverage for sibling component calls, handler calls, and parameter shadowing.

## IMPACT
