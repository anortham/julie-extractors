## T6 measured the crossover; repairing the perf harness exposed a pre-existing regression

Third and final chunk on `delta-resolution-soundness`. Branch now at 2c0deb8, 6 commits, nothing pushed.

### The perf harness was dead, not just broken
`resolution_perf.rs` stopped COMPILING when `reference_sites` became a NOT NULL FK on `identifiers`, `relationships`, AND `pending_relationships` — the seed fixture inserted all three without one. Every arm died at seed time. So the 150ms single-file ceiling has been enforcing nothing. Fixture now writes an exact site per row (`is_exact=1` + `provenance='target_token'` + all six spans, per the CHECK).

### T6 — and the trap in measuring it
**The sweep cannot measure the crossover while the crossover is live.** Past the threshold, every point times a Full pass and reports it as the scoped cost, so the sweep only rediscovers its own input. My first reading "confirmed" 0.5 exactly this way. Added `resolve_workspace_with_crossover` (threaded param, NOT an env var — perf tests run in parallel and `set_var` is unsafe in 2024 edition) so the sweep can pass 2.0 and keep the scoped path scoped.

Honest curve (release, 2000 files / 92k identifiers, FULL=648ms):
`n=1 0.27x | n=50 0.31x | n=500 (25%) 0.57x | n=1000 (50%) 0.86x | n=1200 (60%) 0.97x | n=1400 (70%) 1.17x | n=2000 1.55x`

Crossing is 60–70% ⟹ **threshold moved 0.5 → 0.6**. Verified the assert bites (fails at 0.8). Assert is one-sided on purpose: promoting early is safe.
