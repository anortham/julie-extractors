## Reviewed the structural-fact registry module split

The split is structurally sound and all focused contract gates pass, but it is not yet merge-ready because two specs crossed the wrong ownership boundary and changed a public accessor's observable order.

- **What:** Reviewed the uncommitted split from `structural_fact_registry.rs` into six family modules against the implementation plan.
- **Why:** The plan requires zero behavior change and collector-aligned family ownership.
- **How:** Miller inspection and reference tracing proved `htmx.attribute.v1` and `alpine.directive.v1` are emitted by `framework_structural_facts`, while the new registry places them in `web.rs`. Exact sequence comparison proved `structural_fact_pattern_specs()` changed from `htmx, alpine, blazor, razor` to `blazor, razor, htmx, alpine`.
- **Impact:** Move the two specs into `framework.rs` before Blazor/Razor and add a public-accessor ordering regression before merge. Also remove explanatory comments from the new layout test to comply with the repository's zero-comments-in-tests rule.
