## Fixed structural-fact registry split review findings

The registry split had moved htmx and Alpine specs into the web family, changing the observable order of the public registry accessor despite the zero-behavior-change requirement.

- **Root cause:** `all_specs()` appends the framework slice before the web slice, so moving framework-emitted specs into `web.rs` reordered them after Blazor/Razor.
- **Fix:** Moved `htmx.attribute.v1` and `alpine.directive.v1` into `framework.rs` immediately before Blazor/Razor, and corrected the accessor-order documentation.
- **Regression:** Added `structural_fact_pattern_specs_preserve_framework_markup_order` through the public accessor; RED returned htmx, Alpine, then CSS, and GREEN restored htmx, Alpine, Blazor, Razor.
- **Policy cleanup:** Removed explanatory comments from the new layout test.
- **Impact:** Collector ownership and the pre-split public sequence are restored without changing serialized contract JSON.
