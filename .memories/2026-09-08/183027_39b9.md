## Core-scaled extraction workers and deep chunk

### WHAT
- `extraction.rs`: `effective_extraction_workers(jobs)` and `default_workers_for(cores)` = cores*4/5 with floor 1; used by the scan pool (commands.rs) and the executor pool.
- `executor.rs`: `frozen_chunk_versions(jobs)` replaces `frozen_chunk_versions_from_environment()`; deep default = `deep_chunk_versions_for_workers(workers)` = max(8, workers). Serde defaults for old payloads stay 100/8.
- `docs/contracts/cli.md`: jobs text and chunk-default text updated.
- Tests: unit tests for both rules; `store_import_contract` deep-chunk test split into `--jobs 1` (8-file chunks) and `--jobs 12` (12-file chunk).

### NUMBERS (paired, this repo, 24 cores)
- import 41.5/40.0 s -> 35.3/35.2 s.
- scan 24 vs 19 workers: 17.4/21.2 s vs 17.5/18.0 s (no loss).
