## Buffered JSONL export writes

- **WHAT:** Added a bounded-write JSONL contract test and buffered `export_jsonl` with a 64 KiB `BufWriter` at the artifact exporter boundary.
- **WHY:** Slice 2 evidence showed export to `/dev/null` spent most time in system calls, pointing at many small writes rather than SQLite reads.
- **HOW:** Followed TDD: the new `buffered_export_uses_bounded_write_calls` test first failed with 2,853 writes for an 8,558-byte export, then passed after wrapping the exporter writer in `BufWriter`.
- **IMPACT:** Release-profile export to `/dev/null` against the same dogfood artifact improved from 20.79s real / 15.66s sys to 2.43s real / 0.21s sys while keeping JSONL and CLI export contracts unchanged.
