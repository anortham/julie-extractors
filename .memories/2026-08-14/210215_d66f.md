## WHAT

Completed the Task 7A producer lifecycle and maintenance recovery slice.

- Added durable generation-fenced temporary rebase pins, retry-safe exact rebase publication, and pin-aware superseded-delta cleanup.
- Added a cross-platform maintenance heartbeat that atomically renews the exact maintenance intent and writer lease across plan validation and apply.
- Added regression contracts for stale pin reuse, CAS crash recovery, pre-CAS failure, short leases during plan validation/apply, and lease takeover.

## WHY

Resolution overlays had grown past two million rows and generic GC could not finish: its 60-second lease expired during an approximately 80-second revalidation and the later multi-minute deletion rolled back.

## HOW

The rebase base is durably pinned before ready/CAS and released only after the view roots it. GC retains atomic store mutation while a background heartbeat renews both coordinator rows under exact run/action/generation/plan/owner/pid/version/token predicates. Renewal loss fails closed and never deletes successor state.

## IMPACT

Production evidence on the copied Miller family passed: one fixed-binary GC retry completed in 336.31s, removed 170 obsolete deltas and 7 stale base files, reduced overlay rows 94.50% and logical resolution bytes 94.16%, left zero eligible parent/child rows, preserved protected roots, and passed all integrity checks.
