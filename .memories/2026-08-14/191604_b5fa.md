## Fixed pathological one-file incremental-resolution routing

### WHAT

Removed the unconditional one-path exemption from `scope_crosses_over`, expanded full/scoped equivalence coverage, added a bounded one-file performance-and-digest oracle, and recorded the dated recovery evidence.

### WHY

A `README.md` change selected 776 versions and 386,163 prior identifier rows, but the exemption bypassed the existing 70% work estimate. Scoped `ResolvedIdentifiers` stayed CPU-bound beyond 600 seconds.

### HOW

- Reused the existing selected-version plus name/receiver identifier estimator for every non-empty scope.
- Kept the threshold, schema, reports, CLI, and public APIs unchanged.
- Proved both sides: broad one-file collisions cross to full; a bounded one-file case stays scoped, completes under 5 seconds, and matches the full oracle digest.
- Replayed the frozen production case: before >600.003 s scoped timeout; after 178.621 s full crossover with exact state.

### IMPACT

Routing correctness is recovered without an always-full shortcut. The production 5-second gate still fails because full resolution consumes 171.167 seconds; retained-history lifecycle and post-rotation reads move to Tasks 7A and 7B.
