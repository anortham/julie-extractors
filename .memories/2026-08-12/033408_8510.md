## WHAT

Added fixed-cardinality SQLite candidate-query telemetry to `StoreScratchResolutionSession` and a focused exact-output regression fixture in `store_resolution_performance.rs`.

## WHY

A real scoped resolve took 520 seconds and produced about 98 GB of logical reads. The immediate theory was repeated-name/high-fanout candidate paging, so Task 1 needed statement-execution and returned-row evidence before any cache change.

## HOW

- Added 14 stable `CandidateQueryFamily` variants covering every one of the 12 `with_candidate_reader` call sites; shared paged lookup distinguishes by-name, children, and top-level shapes.
- Count only statements that actually execute; cache hits do not count.
- Added a 32-identifier/301-candidate fixture that verifies 32 exact ambiguous outputs and known counters: PrimeWindow 1/300, FilteredNameSummary 1/2, IdentifierHydration 1/32.
- Used Miller `trace with_candidate_reader` as the completeness checklist.

## IMPACT

The synthetic fixture disproved both per-identifier TopLevelNamed and FilteredNameSummary amplification. Task 2 must consume the new complete family telemetry on a faithful scoped replay to find the actual 520-second query family instead of implementing a speculative cache.
