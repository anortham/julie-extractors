## Selected the measured resolver fix

- **WHAT:** Updated the query-amplification design and plan after one faithful 49.81-second scoped replay.

- **HOW:** Fixed-family telemetry showed LocateIdentifier at 10,804 of 14,980 executions (72.12%), exactly matching pending rows.
