## WHAT

Hardened the Task 2 test-only diagnostic so a killed 60-second replay preserves live fixed-family counters instead of writing evidence only after resolution returns.

## WHY

Lead review found that the initial worker emitted counters only after `run_resolution_session`; a timeout would lose exactly the evidence the diagnostic exists to capture.

## HOW

- Added `SnapshottingSession`, an integration-test-only `ResolutionSession` delegate around `StoreScratchResolutionSession`.
- It persists the complete fourteen-family sample after phase chunks, resolved edges, and flushes when total statement executions cross exponentially growing thresholds.
- Persistence writes a pending JSON file and atomically renames it to `<output>.live.json`, limiting measurement distortion and preventing torn snapshots.
- Added an exact test proving a live snapshot exists before `finish_exact` and records PrimeWindow 1 execution/300 rows.

## IMPACT

Future bounded scoped replay timeouts will retain a valid last-known counter sample without changing production or the public CLI. The prior timed-out fixture setup was not rerun and remains preserved at `/tmp/julie-store-query-replay-20260811`.
