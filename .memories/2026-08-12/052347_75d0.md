## Rejected Pending co-location batching after faithful replay

A strict RED/GREEN proved that enriching each bounded Pending page could remove all 10,804 per-row locator statements while preserving exact span, line, ambiguity, no-match, and non-store fallback semantics.

- **What:** The experiment reduced total candidate statements from 14,980 to 4,176 and `LocateIdentifier` from 10,804 to zero.
- **Why rejected:** The one permitted faithful replay did not improve time: resolver moved from 24,813ms to 25,418ms and process wall from 49.88s to 50.46s.
- **How handled:** Manually removed the entire uncommitted Pending wrapper, store join, resolver fallback, and mechanism regression. Production source and tests now match HEAD `82c77ff8`.
- **Impact:** Query-count amplification was real but not wall-clock load-bearing on this host. Do not reintroduce this slice. Continue from measured time in `IdentifierHydration`, `PrimeWindow`, or the separate roughly 24-second exact finalization path.
