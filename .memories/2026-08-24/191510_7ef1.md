## WHAT

Made the maintenance crash-test helper accept an explicit lease duration. The store-commit crash worker now uses 5,000 ms; the coordinator-recovery worker retains 250 ms.

## WHY

CI scheduling and SQLite contention exhausted the store worker's 250 ms lease before it reached `maintenance_store_after_commit`, producing `MaintenanceFenceLost` instead of the intended crash hook. The coordinator test intentionally relies on the 250 ms abandoned lease expiring before a 300 ms retry, so a global lease increase broke that separate contract.

## HOW

Systematic debugging compared CI stderr, crash-hook ordering, heartbeat renewal timing, and working lease patterns. The first global change exposed the coordinator coupling; the corrected helper parameter preserves both independent test intents without production changes.

## IMPACT

Both store atomicity and coordinator recovery crash contracts are deterministic under their appropriate lease budgets. The exact candidate must be repushed and CI must pass before tagging.
