## WHAT

Completed accumulated delta resolution rebase logic in julie-extract 2.33.3.

- Store scope planning validates transition history and computes the unique current-manifest identifiers touched across selected, name, and receiver arms.
- More than one validated transition and strict unique coverage above 25% triggers one exact rebase through the existing validated rebase path.
- The trigger remains private telemetry and does not change the public report contract.

## WHY

Long sequences of individually small deltas accumulated repeated resolution work and could make later updates progressively expensive.

## HOW

Deduplicated current-manifest identifier keys across all scoped arms, preserved the existing 70% single-delta crossover, and required artifact rebase proof validation before honoring the accumulated-work marker. Added exact-oracle parity, sequence, boundary, crash/replay, and scale performance coverage.

## IMPACT

A 79-transition fixture with 33.054% unique coverage rebased once, left zero delta rows, preserved exact output, and did not rebase again during three one-file warm updates. Overall warm p95 was 925ms.
