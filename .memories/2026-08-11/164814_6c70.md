## Third review pass fixed the plan's equivalence gates and seam claims

A Claude verification pass at `692ef4e` re-proved every ground-truth claim in
`docs/plans/2026-08-11-store-incremental-resolution-brief.md` with two parallel code-verification
agents, then applied corrections to the brief and its HTML digest.

### WHAT

- Replaced every `catalog_hash` equivalence gate with a canonical semantic row digest
  (ordered dumps of `resolution_base_versions`, `identifier_resolutions`, `pending_resolutions`).
- Named three manifest-seam bypasses in ground truth and Task 2: `apply_forward_rollback`
  (`store/generation.rs:1061`, `:1121`), promotion's verbatim `views` copy
  (`logical_copy_generation`, `:611`), and the direct exact-binding write at CLI
  `store/executor.rs:1157` (moves behind `ResolutionBindingStore`; Task 2 now owns `executor.rs`).
- Marked the 151.7 MB gap-growth figure as an unrecorded session measurement; Task 7 thresholds are
  provisional and Task 8 re-measures and records them. Corrected the 2m24s framing (full-corpus
  replay after a 98-file delta; 98 is the gap-file count).
- Added a journal write bound: header-only scope-unusable batches when no scope state exists or the
  change count exceeds a fixed bound.
- Precision fixes: four inert phases (`ResolvedPending`, `PropagationCovered`,
  `ResolvedIdentifiers`, `PropagationOwned` at `resolution_session.rs:1488-1496`), identifier key is
  `(version_id, identifier_id)` not `(version_id, local_id)`, `store_meta` journal-version read must
  tolerate an absent key, `has_ready_base` at `resolve.rs:235`, `publish_exact` at
  `store/resolution.rs:1258`.

### WHY

`resolution_base_catalog_hash` (`store/resolution.rs:3713`) hashes only normalized `sqlite_master`
DDL. Every "catalog_hash equality" gate in the plan would always pass and prove nothing about row
content. The legacy oracle (`resolution_scope_equivalence.rs:166`) compares full row dumps — the
store suite now mirrors it.

### HOW

Two parallel read-only verification agents checked all 21 ground-truth claims (CLI side and
artifact side); I confirmed the base-file DDL, the schema-only hash, and the legacy row-dump oracle
directly. All corrections were folded into the brief with a new "Claude verification-pass
disposition" section, and the HTML digest's three stale bullets were updated.

### IMPACT

The plan's ship gates now actually test scoped-versus-full equivalence. Task 1 can start without
workers trusting a hash that cannot fail or a seam claim that misses three writers.
