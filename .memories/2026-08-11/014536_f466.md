## WHAT

Repaired versioned family-store resolution performance in `julie-extract-cli` while preserving exact output and the 300-row bounded-memory contract.

- Reused one validated store reader and prepared target-existence statement during exact-base finish.
- Added selective candidate lookup by language, kind, and optional source version.
- Bounded candidate-name/module caches and high-water instrumentation; high-cardinality children, type facts, and imports now stream without retention.
- Reused one reader across phase freezes and candidate hydration, batched relationship identifier hydration, and spilled tier candidate deduplication only after the in-memory window fills.
- Added production-cardinality regressions and a public finding/plan digest.

## WHY

A Miller-repository store resolution replay took 6:20.55 inside a roughly nine-minute end-to-end convergence. The dominant causes were per-target validated reader opens, broad repeated name scans, per-edge scratch writes, and repeated reader/query setup.

## HOW

The repair keeps resolver policy storage-agnostic through `CandidateLookup` and changes only the store adapter's query/lifetime strategy. A speculative per-source-file snapshot reached 1:54.02 but was rejected because it could issue one discarded query per source version and complicated the bounded-memory proof. The mergeable design uses bounded batch reads and completed the same replay in 2:24.54 at 90,216 KB RSS.

## IMPACT

The real replay is 2.6x faster than the 6:20.55 producer baseline and preserves 37,965 exact gaps across 98 files for 416,361 visible identifiers. Default, contract, strict clippy, formatting, mechanism, and performance gates pass. Full-corpus resolution remains a separate incremental-architecture opportunity; Miller's zero-change sidecar rewrite is repaired in a separate Miller branch.
