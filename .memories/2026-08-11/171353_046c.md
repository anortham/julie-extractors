## Implemented schema-v2 resolution-scope lifecycle journal

### WHAT

Added the three-table scope journal, typed lifecycle records/readers/validation, atomic feature installation, and transition capture under the private manifest publication seam. Added focused schema, connection, manifest, and generation-promotion coverage plus updated store contracts and the catalog authority hash.

### WHY

Incremental resolution needs a trustworthy artifact-side record of the first exact predecessor and every later manifest transition. Manifest generations can be reused, so they cannot identify transitions; legacy or discontinuous history must force full fallback.

### HOW

- `transition_id` is SQLite `AUTOINCREMENT`; `previous_transition_id` preserves view-local order.
- The first exact manifest/base/delta/resolver-epoch tuple is preserved across later unbound transitions.
- Touched paths are binary ordered and hashed with nullable old/new version IDs.
- A 512-change bound matches the existing generation-copy default window; missing history/state, discontinuity, and 513+ changes produce immutable scope-unusable headers with no children.
- Read-only legacy schema-v2 stores tolerate absent metadata. Writer open/publication installs feature version 1 without changing `user_version=2`.
- Promotion reaches writer upgrade before catalog comparison and copies the journal rows/metadata through existing generic generation-copy code.

### IMPACT

Task 2 can consume a durable validated scope chain without adding journal policy to CLI executors. GC/rooting, rollback/promotion bypass semantics, and exact-publication clearing remain explicitly owned by Task 2.
