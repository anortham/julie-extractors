- **Fix:** Added crate-private `retire_resolution_scope_chain` and routed all winning exact paths plus forward rollback through it.

- **Fresh chains:** Adjusted installed-feature scope capture ordering so the first post-exact manifest can establish a new predecessor without a prior batch, while legacy bootstrap remains header-only full fallback.
- **GC:** Maintenance prunes batches for views without durable scope state before reclaiming manifests/versions.
