## WHAT

Implemented and lead-approved Task 6 end-to-end store resolution wiring, additive telemetry, deterministic full/scoped equivalence oracles, and stale-CAS protection.

## WHY

Scoped execution must remain explicitly opt-in and prove exact semantic equivalence before becoming the default. Terminal reporting also must survive crashes without misrepresenting the executed decision or changing existing schema-v1 counts.

## HOW

`JULIE_STORE_RESOLUTION_DELTA=on|off` is strict and defaults off. Base builds and off/unset exact runs bypass predecessor reads entirely; on-mode delegates the sole scope decision to the store session. The winning exact publication atomically embeds strictly validated telemetry in its store-log effect, scoped by request/view/generation, and retry replays that telemetry plus committed delta/gap counts. Partial telemetry fails closed while legacy effects with no telemetry remain readable. Curated hazards and three seeded four-generation sequences compare canonical semantic SHA-256 digests; tests assert real scoped execution, intended crossover fallback, genuine unchanged-version sibling carry-forward, and no stale effect or terminal after CAS loss.

## IMPACT

Tasks 1–7 remain forced-full by default while explicit on-mode is now end-to-end, observable, crash-safe, and evidence-backed. Task 7 can add rebase below the same publication fence without changing the differential oracle.
