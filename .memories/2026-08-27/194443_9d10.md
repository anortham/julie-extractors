## Audited continuous-testing language readiness

### WHAT

Compared Miller continuous-testing contracts with all 39 julie-extractors language capability records and golden evidence.

- Current Miller provider groups: .NET, Rust, Python, JavaScript, and QML.
- All present source-language extractor candidates are partial under the strict proof standard.
- F# has no source extractor.
- R has an unsupported lifecycle applicability claim.
- Framework-local gaps include Go `t.Run`, Scala parameterized and lifecycle roles, and several secondary frameworks.

### WHY

The project needs proof that extractor facts can support each new Miller provider before provider work starts.

### HOW

Used cross-workspace Miller indexes, capability rows, golden fixtures, provider code, impact selection code, and independent adversarial review.

Separated extractor gaps from Miller provider defects and external runtime proof.

### IMPACT

Do not start a broad extractor rewrite. First strengthen the five-role evidence contract and stable identity or selector proof.

The quickest Miller language-count gains are likely Visual Basic, JSX, and TSX after Miller mapping fixes. Go is the best first new provider ecosystem after focused extractor proof.

Miller must first fix false `KnownEmpty` results, detailed role loss, provider-to-symbol identity, and language-family mapping.
