## Locked parser and grammar freshness design

The completed T-SQL branch now has a durable follow-on design for Tree-sitter `0.26.11`, current C# 14/.NET file-based apps, Swift `0.7.3`, R `1.3.0`, and automated drift reporting.

- **Why:** Published version numbers did not expose the T-SQL or current C# syntax gaps.
- **How:** Keep parser migrations language-local, prove them through canonical extraction, and isolate network metadata behind a non-default script.
- **Impact:** Establishes the approved architecture and acceptance bar for the implementation plan and Claude review.
