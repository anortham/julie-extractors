## Designed current-schema writer performance guard

- **WHAT:** Drafted the approved design for TODO item 6 in `docs/plans/2026-06-09-writer-current-schema-performance-design.md`.
- **WHY:** The SQLite writer has fast tripwires and end-to-end dogfood baselines, but lacks a non-default large current-schema guard that exercises every v2 child-row domain.
- **HOW:** Chose a new `cargo xtask performance writer-current-schema` command that generates deterministic synthetic artifact rows, writes a real SQLite database under `target/`, and emits summary JSON with row totals, elapsed time, rows/sec, and artifact size.
- **IMPACT:** Locks the first backlog slice to evidence tooling only, keeping schema-changing work for items 7, 8, and 9 separate.
