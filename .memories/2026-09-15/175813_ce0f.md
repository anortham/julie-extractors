## facts level keeps literals and type-usage/member-access identifiers (feat/facts-level)

**WHAT:** `ExtractionLevel::includes_identifiers()` (facts and full) now gates the 14 per-language identifier walks in `registry.rs`. `strip_to_level(Facts)` retains identifiers of kind `TypeUsage` and `MemberAccess`, keeps literals, clears type-argument usages and source regions. Tests updated: lib `facts_level_keeps_structural_facts_literals_and_type_and_member_identifiers_for_every_fixture_language`, `facts_level_fixtures_actually_carry_type_or_member_identifiers`, CLI `scan_level_facts_keeps_facts_literals_and_type_and_member_identifiers`. CLI contract, help text, and 2.43.0 release notes updated.

**WHY:** code-kb `find_references` needs type usages and member accesses to answer "who uses this type or field". The user picked this over full identifiers (about 1 GB more on hermes-agent) or none.

**HOW:** The identifier walk runs at facts, so literals are now uniform across languages and are kept. Measured on hermes-agent: 52 s, 2.6 GB RSS, 1.7 GB (full: 85 s, 5.3 GB, 3.9 GB).

**IMPACT:** `facts` semantics changed before any publication; nothing released yet.
