## vbnet: fixed second review of call-initializer type inference

- **Partial types:** a type with the Partial modifier, or one with more than one part in the file, now records no fact from `Load()`, `Me.Load()`, or `Split.Make()`. Another part, maybe in another file, can add an overload. The field `is_open` became `is_split` plus an `is_open()` method.
- **Shared-call qualifier:** the enclosing types are walked from the innermost outward. A member with the qualifier name stops the walk (no fact). A nested type with that name ends the walk (bound). An open type stops the walk (no fact).
- **Module members:** a qualifier that names a member of an in-scope module records no fact (`module_declares` replaced `enclosing_declares`).
- **Const:** `add_type` indexes `const_declaration` names. `is_member_scope_name` in locals.rs also matches local `Const` (SymbolKind::Constant).
- **Parentheses:** `shape_of` unwraps `parenthesized_expression`, so `(Await F())` and `Await (F())` are inferred.
- **Corpus:** no change to the inferred facts (samples 87, docs 183). The proxy unresolved counts are unchanged.
