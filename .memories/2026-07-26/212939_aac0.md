## WHAT

Implemented the schema-5 canonical reference-site contract across Julie extraction, normalization, SQLite, JSONL, reports, release guards, and documentation.

## WHY

Miller and Eros need one authoritative physical-site identity without reconstructing target tokens from names, lines, or overlapping spans.

## HOW

- Exact site IDs derive from `(file_id,start_byte,end_byte)` only when the provider attests the target token.
- Spanless rows are row-specific and explicitly `is_exact=false`.
- Identifier, relationship, pending, and resolution evidence carries the canonical site ID.
- Resolved assertions preserve `(site,target_symbol,canonical_kind)` and unresolved assertions preserve `(site,target_name,canonical_kind)`.
- Capability-gap status is closed to `open | exception`, with exactly 70 certified open gaps.
- SQLite schema/catalog, extracted-data, and JSONL contracts moved to 5/4/4 with no compatibility aliases.

## IMPACT

Consumers can trust exact sites and stop inferring them. Broad provider gaps remain visible and non-authoritative until each producer is audited.
