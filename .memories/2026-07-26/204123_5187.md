## Implemented extractor-owned `code.marker.v1` facts

Julie now emits one generic structural fact for each actionable physical comment or doc-comment line instead of requiring consumers to rescan raw text.

- **Why:** Marker classification belongs with parser-backed source regions and must behave consistently across every supported language.
- **How:** A shared line-oriented collector consumes canonical `SourceRegion` rows, strips comment decoration, requires `TODO`, `FIXME`, `HACK`, or `XXX` as the first semantic token, and preserves the exact marker-through-line-end span.
- **Contract:** Facts carry normalized `marker`, optional `owner`, optional `description`, and `source_region_kind` metadata; regex is explicitly not applicable.
- **Registry:** Registered the pattern for all 35 comment-capable languages and regenerated the machine-readable structural-fact contract.
- **Impact:** Miller can consume authoritative marker facts without language-specific or raw-text rescanning.
