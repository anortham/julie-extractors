## WHAT

Repaired the canonical extraction goldens after target-token relationship attestation changed audited relationship spans.

## WHY

The integrated contract gate correctly rejected stale expectations that still described enclosing call-expression spans. The golden projection also failed to record the new typed exactness field.

## HOW

- Added `reference_site_is_exact` to `NormalizedRelationship` and populated it from the public producer model.
- Regenerated expected outputs through the repository's `UPDATE_GOLDEN=1` test path.
- Audited the generated set: 101 fixture files, 224 relationship rows, 14 exact and 210 non-exact.
- Verified the only pre-existing-value changes are narrowed target-token spans in audited Bash, C, PowerShell, and Python fixtures; other fixture changes add the boolean contract field.

## IMPACT

The contract gate now checks both target-token spans and typed exactness across the full golden matrix. Marker behavior and production attestation code are unchanged.
