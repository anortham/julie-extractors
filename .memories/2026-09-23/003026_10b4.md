## C# wave-2 extraction gaps closed (gaps/dotnet)

- **WHAT:** Closed all 25 csharp wave-2 gaps. Records now give public properties. `params` parameters, pattern designations, and extension receivers are now parameter or local variables. Using aliases keep their targets. Attribute targets become annotation carriers. Bare calls bind to the enclosing type first. A new C# preprocessor pass blanks directive lines and `#else` branches. Type facts now come from the syntax tree only. TUnit, SpecFlow hooks, and MSpec get test roles. ASP.NET gains verb joins, AcceptVerbs, endpoint maps, nested groups, and `aspnet.conventional_route.v1`. EF Core gains `efcore.db_set.v1`, `efcore.table_mapping.v1`, and `efcore.entity_configuration.v1`.
- **WHY:** These were wave-2 gaps from docs/plans/2026-09-22-language-gap-closure.md.
- **HOW:** New modules: csharp/scope.rs, csharp/preprocessor.rs, framework_structural_facts/efcore.rs, and aspnet_conventional.rs. `infer_types` now copies only recorded facts.
- **IMPACT:** The open gaps specflow and mspec were removed. The narrower gap `specflow.step_definition_role` was added. Next: the F# gaps.
