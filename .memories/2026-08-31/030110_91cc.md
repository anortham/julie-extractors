### WHAT

Added grammar-backed F# source regions for line/block/XML-doc comments and string forms, registered `fsharp.attribute.v1`, attached attribute facts to annotated symbols, and added an explicit policy so scalar F# literals survive artifact mapping.

### WHY

The F# capability row had `source_regions` and `structural_facts` open gaps, making strict data quality report two debts. Canonical extraction emitted literals, but the CLI artifact policy discarded all carrier-less literals, leaving the pinned Expecto evidence at zero.

### HOW

- Grammar inspection of the pinned `tree-sitter-fsharp` 0.3.0 node types confirmed `line_comment`, `block_comment`, `xml_doc`, `string`, `triple_quoted_string`, `verbatim_string`, `bytearray`, `verbatim_bytearray`, and `attribute`.
- Structural facts use standard `pattern_version=1` / `query_family=metadata` metadata and confidence 1.0.
- Regenerated F# goldens and the structural-fact contract from source-of-truth registries.
- Rebuilt/rescanned the detached Expecto checkout at commit `cec2c63c8d77c6c21bf7e35d903020f74ddc1cea`.

### IMPACT

Strict quality is now `silent_cells=0`, `quality_bar_debts=0`. F# goldens publish source regions and attribute facts, and the artifact scan publishes 4,014 F# literals instead of zero. Remaining F# gaps are top-level script imports, non-attribute domain-native patterns, test containers/lifecycle, and unsupported Expecto/NUnit/FsUnit roles.
