## WHAT

Implemented the approved BRE-42 Task 3 F# xUnit role slice. Added `fsharp/test_detection.rs`, wired it into `FSharpExtractor::extract_symbols`, and added focused F# tests for Fact, Theory, qualified xUnit markers, and negative controls.

## WHY

The F# extractor now needs to publish the shared test-role contract for xUnit callables while avoiding false positives for similarly named attributes, non-callable declarations, unannotated functions, and unsupported test ecosystems.

## HOW

- Reused the shared `apply_test_role` writer so `is_test`, lifecycle/container flags, and `test_role` remain consistent.
- Matched only exact normalized keys: `fact`, `xunit.fact`, `global.xunit.fact`, `theory`, `xunit.theory`, and `global.xunit.theory`.
- Restricted classification to Function, Method, and Constructor symbols.
- Followed RED/GREEN TDD: the three tests compiled with two positive failures before production code, then all focused and F# module tests passed.

## IMPACT

F# xUnit Fact and Theory declarations now emit `test_case` and `parameterized_test`; unsupported Expecto/NUnit/FsUnit/container/lifecycle forms remain unclassified for the later evidence/capability task. The owned slice is ready for the serial worker commit.
