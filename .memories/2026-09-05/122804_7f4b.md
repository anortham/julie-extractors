## What

Implemented standalone family-store completion checkpoints in CLI store dispatch and new completion.rs, based on J1 ecd021c0. No J1 source worktree edits.

## Why

Command completion relied on SQLite automatic/last-close cleanup, which left WALs behind while readers stayed open. Miller cannot own standalone producer cleanup.

## How

After mutating commands finish, resolve current StoreLayout and attempt both DB checkpoints with 250ms busy wait each, no CREATE. Consume busy status and emit remaining-byte diagnostics on stderr without changing stdout or committed outcome. Read-only commands/dry-runs excluded. Next mutation, replay or no-change retries.
