## SQLite write-path optimization complete (perf/sqlite-write-path)

Optimized the SQLite artifact write path, which became the cold-scan bottleneck (~80-85% of scan time) after extraction was parallelized. All work committed on branch `perf/sqlite-write-path` (sits on top of `perf/parallel-extraction` @ 8a63834).

### What landed before review fixes (5 commits)
- **f1df011** Step 1 — fold identifier FK resolution into the INSERT (drop the two-step insert-then-UPDATE for containing/target).
- **9717d50** Step 2 — reuse the existing-file lookup across spooled passes (kill the duplicate `load_existing_file` SELECT per file).
- **0b32437** Step 3 — collect symbol ids during the insert pass (4 spool deserializations → 3; simplification, confirmed serde is not the cost).
- **6ba1d48** Step 5 — tried skipping FK enforcement during the spooled bulk write via `ForeignKeysOff` RAII guard; post-review regression coverage found this breaks SQLite `ON DELETE` null/cascade actions for surviving cross-file rows, so the optimization was reverted and FK enforcement remains on.
- **8b316d0** Step 4 — fold symbol parent resolution into the INSERT (drop pass D / `update_symbol_parent_rows` on the spooled path).

### Impact (clean interleaved same-session A/B, release, cold force scan)
- **openclaw**: artifact_write 31,942 → 18,728ms median (**−41%**); end-to-end scan 38.0s → 24.5s.
- **hermes**: artifact_write 10,956 → 6,639ms median (**−39%**); end-to-end scan 13.7s → 9.3s.
- **Output byte-identical**: row counts AND resolved-FK columns match exactly on both corpora (openclaw ids=1,102,973 syms=640,317 parent=298,576 containing=1,087,006 target=0; identical baseline vs final).

### Rejected by measurement
- **Step 6** (durability pragmas: journal_mode=MEMORY + synchronous=OFF) was SLOWER (24,016ms vs 22,500ms). fsync is not the bottleneck; reverted.
- Honors prior backed-out negatives: bulk index drop/rebuild and existing-row cache both regressed real openclaw earlier and were NOT reintroduced.

### Scope discipline
- Only the spooled cold-scan path was changed. In-memory batch (`write_scan_snapshot`) and single-file update (`write_files`) paths keep full FK enforcement + insert-then-update parent (not the bottleneck) — `insert_symbols(file, None)` preserves their behavior.
- 24 writer_contract + 2 writer_performance tests green; schema_contract green (fresh writer reports foreign_keys=1).
